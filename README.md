Solmar Bay is an original open-world crime game that runs in a desktop browser from static files. Dani Reyes, a getaway driver, returns to his coastal hometown to buy his sister Lila's freedom from the Coral Kings for $2,600 — across 8 story missions, on foot and by stolen car, with the Solmar Bay police on his tail.

The city has a road grid with traffic and 26 pedestrians, 28 cars to steal, a five-star wanted system with police cruisers that spawn around the player, punch combat, 12 hidden sunset coins, taxi fares, a live minimap, 4 synthesized radio stations, and a save that carries progress between sessions. Nothing in it comes from any commercial game: the city, names, characters, story, art and music are all original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 and press START ENGINE. WASD moves and drives, E enters and leaves a car, F or Space punches, Shift sprints, M starts the next mission, T takes a taxi fare, R changes the radio, 1–4 pick a station, N mutes, P pauses.
